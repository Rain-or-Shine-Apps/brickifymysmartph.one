How it works: five steps

1

Choose a passcode

Set a 4-digit PIN you'll remember but won't enter on impulse.

2

Set a second factor

Parent PIN, authenticator app, or email code. Both factors are required to unbrick — losing one doesn't lock you out forever.

3

Save your recovery codes

Three one-time backup codes are generated. Write them down. If you ever lose your PIN and second factor, one of these codes will get you back in.

4

Choose which apps to block, and set up the blocking schedules too

Pick exactly which apps are disabled when bricked. Everything else stays accessible.

5

Tap BRICK

Your chosen apps are blocked at the OS level. To unblock them, you need both your PIN and your second factor.

Brickify vs Screen Time and Family Link

Both platforms already have a built-in way to restrict a phone. Here's what Brickify adds on top, platform by platform.

iPhone

Compared to: Apple Screen Time & Family Sharing

1

Profiles that switch themselves

Set up profiles like "School Day" or "Weeknight," each with its own blocked-app list, and Brickify swaps between them automatically on schedule. No manually reconfiguring Screen Time's Downtime or App Limits every time the situation changes.

2

An email if something's wrong

If the phone misses a scheduled check-in, you'll hear about it, rather than assuming everything's still fine.

3

Hardened against removal

Family Controls restrictions stay enforced at the OS level even if Brickify itself is deleted, and an optional Screen Time passcode set up in Brickify's own wizard can block deletion entirely. See known limitations & accepted risks →

4

Clearer to set up

One guided wizard, start to finish, versus juggling Screen Time, Family Sharing, and getting restrictions to actually stick to a child's own device rather than yours.

Android

Compared to: Google Family Link & Digital Wellbeing

1

Profiles that switch themselves

Set up profiles like "School Day" or "Weeknight," each with its own blocked-app list, and Brickify swaps between them automatically on schedule. No manual reconfiguring needed as the day goes on.

2

Richer alerts

Email or notification if the phone stops checking in, if Device Owner protection gets removed, if it boots into Safe Mode, or if a new app appears despite blocking. Family Link surfaces none of these.

3

Hardened against removal

Device Owner mode has no Settings toggle at all. The only ways off are a full factory reset, or Brickify's own PIN and second-factor deactivation. See known limitations & accepted risks →

4

Clearer to set up

Instead of a new, separate, supervised Google account and Family Link's own settings, one wizard covers everything, schedules and block lists, in one place. A factory reset is required, but you'd likely do that before handing a phone to your child anyway.
