Check the phone will work

“Will the drawer phone work? 30 seconds to find out.”

15 October 2026 · By Anne Mundy · about 1 min

← Back to Parenting & phones

That old phone in the kitchen drawer might be your child's first phone. Before you plan anything around it, check it can run what it needs to.

Do it

  1. iPhone: Settings → General → About → Software Version. You need iOS 16 or later, which means iPhone 8 / iPhone X or newer.
  2. Android: Settings → About phone → Android version. You need Android 8.0 or later, which is almost anything from about 2018 onwards.
  3. Showing an older number? Look in Settings → General → Software Update (iPhone) or Settings → System update (Android). Many phones can update further than the version they're on.

While you're holding it

Look at the battery and the screen. On an iPhone, Settings → Battery → Battery Health tells you how tired the battery is. A cracked screen or a battery that dies by lunchtime is worth sorting now, not on Christmas morning.
