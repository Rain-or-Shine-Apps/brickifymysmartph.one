The 12 days steps of Christmas · Step 11 of 12

Lock it in

“The step most people skip.”

15 October 2026 · By Anne Mundy · about 15 mins

← Back to Parenting & phones

Everything so far can be undone by a child who deletes the app. This is the step that stops that, and it's the one most people skip.

iPhone

  1. Settings → Screen Time → Use Screen Time Passcode. Use a code your child doesn't know. You can reuse your Brickify PIN digits.
  2. Turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions, then set Deleting Apps to Don't Allow.

The full walkthrough: iOS setup guide.

Android

Use Device Owner mode. It makes Brickify impossible to switch off or uninstall. It starts with a factory reset, so have a second device handy for the instructions. Follow the Device Owner setup guide.

Both: add an alert email

Brickify emails you if something needs attention: the phone goes silent, its protection gets removed, or a new app appears despite blocking.
