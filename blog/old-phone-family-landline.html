The landline is back. You may already own one

You don't have to buy a special phone to try it. The old one in your drawer can do the job, with no SIM and the apps you choose blocked.

5 November 2026 · By Anne Mundy

← Back to Parenting & phones

The Times has just run a piece called "The must-have landline aimed at the smartphone generation". Parents want their children to be able to ring a friend without being handed the whole internet. You may already own the phone for it: it's the old one in your drawer.

Why parents are going back to the landline

In South Portland, Maine, nearly 20 families have formed a "landline pod". Their parents say the phones help the children listen better and arrange their own playdates.

The appeal is simple: a phone that stays at home and doesn't bring the rest of the internet with it.

How to turn an old phone into a landline

  1. Check the phone. Brickify works on iPhone 8 / iPhone X or newer (iOS 16+) and Android 8.0 or newer.
  2. Wipe it and leave the SIM out. Start with a clean phone, then connect it to your home Wi-Fi.
  3. Install Brickify and choose your PIN. You hold it, so the settings stay yours.
  4. Choose what to block. In the video I'm picking whole categories of apps for a profile. Leave open the app your family will call on.
  5. Start Brickify. The screen changes to "Phone Bricked". Changing the settings takes your PIN.

How calls work with no SIM

No SIM means no phone number, so there are no ordinary calls or texts. Calls go over Wi-Fi in an app instead, such as FaceTime on an iPhone or Google Meet on Android, and whoever they're ringing needs the same app. Set it up with the people they can ring, then test a call before you rely on it.

You can still call 999. The Emergency Call button stays on the bricked screen.

What it won't do

  • It won't work away from your Wi-Fi. Like a landline, it stays at home. If they need to be reachable when they're out, read smartphone-free but still reachable.

The old phone has two things going for it: you already own it, and it can change as they grow. When the time comes, you can add a SIM and open up apps one at a time. That's the phone ladder.
